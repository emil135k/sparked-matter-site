Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Sparked Matter LLC ("we," "us") is an engineering and technology consulting business based in St. Petersburg, Florida. This policy explains what information we collect through sparkedmatter.com and how we handle it.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us:

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email address, optional phone number, and whatever you write in the message field.
  • Appointment scheduling — if you book a consultation through our scheduling link, the name, email, and any details you provide during booking.
  • Direct contact — information you share when you email or call us.

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect payment information through this website.

How We Use It

Your information is used to respond to your inquiry, schedule and conduct consultations, and carry out work you engage us for. That's it. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service Providers

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Contact form submissions and appointment scheduling are handled by third-party services that process your submission in order to deliver it to us. These providers maintain their own privacy policies governing the data they process on our behalf. We use them only to receive and manage inquiries.

Analytics and Cookies

This is a static website. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting and form providers may collect standard technical information such as IP address and browser type as part of normal operation and security.

Confidentiality of Client Information

Technical information you share with us in the course of a consultation — designs, schematics, system details, operational data, or business information — is treated as confidential and is not disclosed to third parties or used outside the scope of your engagement. Where a written confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement is in place, that agreement governs.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry and project correspondence for as long as needed to serve you and to meet ordinary business and recordkeeping needs. You may request deletion of your information at any time.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at the address below and we will respond promptly.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
